AI → SI. NOBODY ASKED.

The domain itself is the punchline. The game turns that premise into an interactive joke.

The premise

AI became SI. That seemed like a bad idea.

FUCKYOUAI.SI imagines the result as a public resistance machine: an industrial-looking target that exists primarily to be punched. The player does not need a technical explanation or a political position. The joke is the transformation of the name and the absurdity of responding to it with an arcade game.

AI

Artificial Intelligence. The familiar label.

SI

Synthetic Intelligence. The deliberately fictional escalation used by the game's world.
