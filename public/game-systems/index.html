THE MACHINE HAS LAYERS.

It looks like a clicker. It is, unfortunately, a clicker with paperwork.

Automation

Micro produces 1 impact per second. Fast produces 2 per second. Slop can produce 5 per second for a limited run and can crash the local run.

Tokens

Tokens begin at a cap of 250 and regenerate over time. Automation consumes tokens; manual punches do not.

Progression

Runs feed permanent progression. XP, levels, unlocks, retraining, builds and cosmetics give repeat sessions a reason to continue without changing the shared global counter into a pay-to-win system.

Daily modifiers

Daily conditions alter the rules or flavor of a session. They include states such as Token Crunch, Hallucination Hour, Context Window, Rate Limit Day and Unlimited Inference.

Challenges & records

Daily challenges, achievements, streaks, personal bests, relay codes, run cards and seasonal records create replayable goals and shareable outcomes.

World & chaos

World events and encounters can temporarily bend the rules. The Chaos area also contains the anonymous global challenge board and endgame records.

Singularity

The long-game end state is a deliberately mysterious Singularity track. It is presented as a forbidden final department rather than an early feature the player has to understand.
