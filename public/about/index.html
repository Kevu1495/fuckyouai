The short version

FUCKYOUAI.SI is an internet-native rage clicker. You punch an AI/SI machine. Every manual hit adds to your local run and contributes to the shared global impact counter through batched synchronization.

The surface is intentionally simple. Underneath it are progression, automation, daily conditions, world events, challenges, achievements, seasonal records, a global chaos board and a long-game Singularity track.

Why the .SI?

The domain is part of the joke. The site's fiction treats AI → SI as a bad development that nobody asked for. The player response is straightforward: punch accordingly.

You do not need to know any outside reference to understand or play the game.

What it is not

It is not an AI assistant, AI model, productivity tool, scientific claim, or serious technology service. It is a free browser game built around a deliberately ridiculous premise.
