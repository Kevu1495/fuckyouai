WELCOME TO THE RESISTANCE DEPARTMENT.

The fiction is simple: the machine is active, the paperwork is questionable, and somebody has to hit it.

Public resistance

FUCKYOUAI.SI is the punching department. It is intentionally louder, more chaotic and more playable than a serious institutional site.

Fake bureaucracy

Game systems are presented as departments, records, registries, campaigns and official-looking machine diagnostics. The serious typography is part of the joke.

Live anomalies

World events temporarily change the atmosphere or rules. Daily events are deterministic for the current UTC day so the headline is shared across players.

Encounters

Later progression can surface machine encounters and unusual states. They are designed to make the simple click loop feel less predictable.

The global record

The shared impact counter is treated like a public statistic: a deliberately meaningless number that gets more ridiculous as everyone contributes.

The forbidden department

Singularity is the late-game track. Its purple/black/acid visual language signals that the machine has moved beyond ordinary paperwork.
